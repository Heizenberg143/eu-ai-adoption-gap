# Europe's AI Adoption Gap

**Data Visualization final project - Summer 2026**  
**Student:** Usaid Aamer

[Open the live Streamlit dashboard](https://usaid-eu-ai-adoption-gap.streamlit.app/)

This project uses official Eurostat enterprise survey data to examine who is
pulling ahead in European AI adoption, where smaller firms fall behind, and
what prevents interested businesses from adopting AI.

## Headline findings

- EU enterprise AI adoption increased from 8.1% in 2023 to 20.0% in 2025.
- Large-firm adoption reached 55.0%, compared with 17.0% for small firms.
- Denmark led the EU at 42.0%; Romania was at 5.2%.
- A lack of expertise was the leading barrier, cited by 70.3% of interested
  non-adopters.
